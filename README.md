# Cardiac Rehab

A personal reference copy of the materials for the **VGH Cardiac Rehabilitation
Program**, run by the Centre for Cardiovascular Health at Vancouver General
Hospital (6th floor, 2775 Laurel Street, Vancouver BC).

This repository is a place to keep the program handouts together, track the
weekly exercise sets as they arrive, and keep notes in one spot instead of
scattered across email attachments.

The manual is organised around the program's **B.E.A.T.T.** goals:

- **B**ehaviours — taking charge of your health and well-being
- **E**ating well and making healthy food choices
- **A**ctivities and exercise to improve fitness and health
- **T**houghts and emotions that may be barriers
- **T**ransition to independent activity and community supports

Chapter outline:

1. Your Physical Activity and Exercise Program
2. Cardiac Health and Medications
3. Healthy Heart Nutrition
4. Taking Care of Mental and Emotional Wellness
5. Your Personal Cardiac Rehab Plan
6. Program Policies

Appendices cover virtual-care privacy, the ACSM intensity and resistance
training guides, Heart & Stroke warning signs for angina and heart attack,
exercise and blood sugar monitoring with diabetes, and a heart-healthy diet
infographic.

Every session follows the same four-part shape:

1. **Warm up** — ~5 minutes of light activity. Not optional.
2. **Aerobic** — trains the cardiovascular and respiratory system. Up to 5 days
   a week; start with 2–3 if you are new to exercise.
3. **Resistance** — trains muscles, joints and bones. 2–3 days a week with at
   least one rest day in between. Runs on a 4-week cycle: bodyweight,
   dumbbells, bands, then balance/posture.
4. **Cool down** — stretching and flexibility, fine to do daily. Also not
   optional.

You can do aerobic and resistance work on separate days and combine them later
as you progress.

## Conventions

- New weekly sets go in `exercises/`, named for the number the program prints
  on the sheet — e.g. `Exercise Set 7.pdf`.
- Reference material that isn't a weekly set goes in `Info/`.
- **Personal health information stays out of this repo.** Activity logs, lab
  results, and case-manager correspondence are covered by `.gitignore`, but the
  ignore rules are a safety net, not a substitute for checking what you commit.

## Important

**This is not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for your program.**
The exercise prescriptions in these documents are individualised — your Case
Manager sets yours based on your own assessment. Do not follow another person's
prescription. During any session, pace yourself, take rest breaks as needed,
stop if you don't feel well, and tell your Case Manager.

If you are experiencing symptoms of a heart attack, call **911**.
